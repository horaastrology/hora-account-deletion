Hora App Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 June 2026

Hora App provides astrology chart, profile, and insight features. This privacy policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how users can request deletion of their account and data.

Information We Collect

Hora App may collect the following information when you use the app:

  • Name, email address, and user ID for sign-in and account management.
  • Birth details, saved astrology profiles, preferences, and app content you create or save.
  • Location information only when you choose to provide it for astrology calculations or personalization.
  • Support messages or deletion requests that you send to us.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide app functionality, manage sign-in and account access, process deletion requests, respond to support requests, improve reliability, maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell user data. We do not share personal data with advertisers. Information may be processed by service providers that support app functionality, authentication, hosting, payments, analytics, or support, only as needed to operate the app and comply with applicable requirements.

Payments and Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription through Google Play, Google processes the payment. Hora App may receive subscription status information needed to unlock premium features, but we do not receive your full payment card details.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data. Data transmitted between the app and our services is protected in transit where applicable.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain user data only as long as needed to provide the app, support users, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

To request deletion, visit the Hora App account deletion page or email bibintb@duck.com.

Children

Hora App is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact bibintb@duck.com.
