# Hora App Account Deletion 
 
This page explains how Hora App users can request deletion of their account and associated app data. 
 
## How to request account deletion 
 
To delete your Hora App account and associated data, send an email to bibintb@duck.com from the same Google account or email address used to sign in to Hora App. 
 
Email subject: Hora App account deletion request 
 
Include your Hora App sign-in email address and a short request saying that you want your Hora App account deleted. 
 
After receiving your request, we will verify account ownership and process the deletion. 
 
## Data deleted 
 
- Hora App account profile 
- Saved astrology profiles 
- Birth chart details saved in the app 
- App preferences linked to your account 
- AI insight history linked to your account where stored 
 
## Data that may be retained 
 
- Support emails and deletion request records 
- Security fraud-prevention legal or compliance records where required 
- Payment or subscription records handled by Google Play where applicable 
 
## Retention period 
 
Account data is normally deleted within 30 days after verification. Records required for legal security tax accounting or compliance reasons may be retained only for as long as required. 
 
## Partial data deletion 
 
You may also request deletion of specific saved profiles or app data without deleting your full account by emailing bibintb@duck.com.
